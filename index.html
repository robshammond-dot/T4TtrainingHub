The Knowledge Hub

One place for everything worth training someone on. Not a filing cabinet, a curated home for what the team actually needs to do the job well.

The idea

Knowledge, not storage

Thompsons For Travel already has a working document store, and that isn't going anywhere. The Knowledge Hub is a different, deliberately smaller thing: a curated space for material that's actually worth training on, destination know-how, sales process, systems guides, onboarding. If it's a working file someone needs for a task, it stays where it already lives. If it's knowledge someone should learn once and come back to, it belongs here.

That distinction is the whole point. A smaller, well-organised hub that people trust is more useful than a large one nobody can find anything in.

Built around

Four things every page does consistently

One look, everywhereSame brand, same navy and cyan, same tone, every page. It should feel like one product, not a patchwork of pages built at different times.
Findable by how people thinkContent is tagged by topic, audience and type, not buried in folders named after whoever built them.
Built for the whole teamThe team isn't deeply technical, so every page favours clarity over cleverness, plain language, obvious next steps, nothing that needs a tutorial of its own.
A living hub, not a launchNew guides, quizzes and destinations get added as the business grows. This is infrastructure, not a one-time project.
The structure

What lives where

Knowledge Hub Home

The landing page. One glance tells you where to go: Onboarding, Training Library, How-to Guides, Training Calendar, Quizzes.

Onboarding

Everything a new starter needs in their first four weeks: welcome pack, week-by-week schedule, systems setup, meeting the team, and the good-to-know essentials.

Training Library

The core of the hub. Every guide, tagged by topic (Destination Knowledge, Sales Process, Systems & Tools, and more), by audience, and by type, so anything can be found in seconds rather than remembered by folder location.

How-to Guides

Step-by-step, task-based help for the systems the team uses daily: FreshChat & the AI Bot, UDMS, Amadeus, FreshSales, and the Ditronics phone system.

Quizzes

Test-your-knowledge tools covering destinations, sales process and systems, including Passport Pro, the team's gamified quiz format.

Training Calendar

Already live and in active use, the team is booking real training sessions on it today.

Resources

Quick links and support contacts for day-to-day questions, the fast-access layer sitting above everything else.

Deliberately outside the hub

What this isn't trying to be

CRM and sales-pipeline documentation lives in its own dedicated space, since most of it isn't relevant to the whole team and it deserves a home suited to that audience specifically. Internal working documents and decision-tracking notes also stay outside the Hub, this is a space for finished, trained-on knowledge, not works in progress.
